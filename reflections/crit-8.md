# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I started the project in DeepSeek Harness, and it spun the backlog off into a
first working version. It followed the plan, but it wasn't getting at what I
wanted: a dashboard of users and a warm "paper" look, when what I had in mind
was much simpler. The breakthrough was changing how I worked with the agent.
Instead of handing it the backlog again, I spun up Claude Code with Opus and
gave it feedback on what I actually wanted: forget the pre-made design, make it
white and minimal like Excalidraw, put me straight on my own board, and let me
select, copy and edit notes. Once I directed it by what I wanted rather than by
the plan, the personal board came together in an evening.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Writing what "good" means for this app showed me that my idea of good is very
personal. I think what I built is good, because it's what I wanted. But good
for other people has to be checked by other people, and that part of the
process is still missing here: so far the only person who has judged the app
is me. I want to be a developer who doesn't stop at "it works for me", and who
asks others early. Next I'll ask my colleagues and my pod to use the board and
tell me what could be better, and let that change what good means in the
README.
