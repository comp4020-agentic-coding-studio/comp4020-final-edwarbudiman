# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was letting other people use the board. At crit 8 I said
the only person who had judged the app was me, and that I wanted to ask
others early. So I tried it myself first, and then my peers tried it during
tutorial time. Their answer was simple: it's okay, but it needs more
features. That was not the praise I hoped for, but it was useful. It told me
the core worked well enough that nobody got stuck on it, and that the next
step was not polishing the same board but giving people more to do on it.
That feedback is what sent me into building more features, and later into
the pipeline that let me build several of them in parallel.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It changed what I count as "done". Before, I judged the app by whether I
liked it. Hearing "it's okay" from peers showed me that working and being
good for other people are not the same thing. I want to be a developer who
puts the work in front of people early, even when it is not finished, and
takes a lukewarm answer as direction instead of as a failure.
