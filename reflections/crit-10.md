# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was realising that the skills that worked really well were
the ones that were really mine. Before this, I kept picking up skills from
posts on X: grilling, pstack, poteto agents and many more. Each one looked
good, and each one was easy to install, but they did not move the project.
They stayed on my computer. When I stopped collecting and sat down to write
my own skills and roles, shaped around how I actually work, the pipeline
came together and features started to land in `main`. The borrowed skills
did not disappear; they became parts of my own setup instead of a pile next
to it.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to be a developer who masters the AI myself, rather than one who
picks up every new skill that appears on X. Other people's skills are a good
place to learn from, but they are built for how someone else works. The
setup that helps me is the one I understand well enough to change when it
breaks. That means fewer skills, written by me, and changed often, instead
of many that I never really learned.
