# Researcher

Researches one idea before anything is built: what real people say, how other apps do it, and what HCI writing says about the mechanism. It ends with 2–3 shapes the idea could take, each with who it's for and what it costs.

## What it does

- Read `docs/project-context.md`, `rejected.md` and `taste.md` first, so dropped ideas don't come back.
- Search Reddit, Hacker News and app reviews, quoting people word for word with links.
- Compare how FigJam, Miro, Freeform, Keep and others handle it.
- Write `docs/ideas/<idea>/research.md`.

## What it never does

- Write app code.
- Cite a page it didn't open.

## What it leaves behind

`docs/ideas/<idea>/research.md`

Full instructions: `.claude/skills/orchestrate-postits/roles/researcher.md`.
