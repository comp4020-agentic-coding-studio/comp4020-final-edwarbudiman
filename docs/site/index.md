# Post-its docs

![A lively board of post-its](/docs/assets/hero.webp)

Post-its is a board for your notes. You put them wherever you like, because **where a note sits is how you prioritise and group it**. People can visit your board and leave you a note, and when they're there at the same time you see their cursor.

**Try it:** [open the app](/) and log in as `demo1` or `demo2` (password `postit-demo`).

- **Features** (left sidebar): what each feature does and how to use it, with a demo.
- **Roles**: the agents (and me) that build each feature, one page each.
- **How it's built**: the pipeline those roles run, and the skills and scripts they use.
- **Versions** (top right): the docs as they were at each milestone.

Why the app is the way it is, and what "good" means for it, is argued in the [README](/readme/).
