# Idea: label-sections

Started 2026-11-09 by the owner.

## Seed

Remove per-note labels. Notes already carry too much (header, text, status footer, sidebar). Replace labels with a separate tool in the toolbar for creating label sections: named areas/regions on the board that group notes by where they sit, instead of tagging each note. Filtering by label should keep working via sections.

## Scope (owner, 2026-11-09)

Small feature. Keep research light: a handful of sources (how FigJam/Miro/Excalidraw do sections or frames, plus what labels in Post-its do today), then 2–3 shapes. No deep literature review.
