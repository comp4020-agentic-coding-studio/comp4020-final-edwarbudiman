# Post-its

A board for your notes. My messages and to-do lists end up scattered and
cluttered, so this is a place to put them as post-its, wherever I like: where
a note sits is how I prioritise and group it. Other people can come to your
board and leave you a note, and when they're there at the same time you see
their cursor, so it feels less lonely.

**Try it:** log in as `demo1` or `demo2`, password `postit-demo`.

## What good means (first version)

For me, good means:

- **It's fully functional.** Log in, land straight on your board, add notes,
  move them, label them, and they're still there when you come back.
- **It's not hard to use.** No dashboard and nothing in the way: you open it
  and you're on your board. Double-click to add or edit a note, drag to move
  it, select several and copy/paste them.
- **People give good feedback when they use it.** I'll check that by watching
  people use it and asking them, starting with my pod at the crit.

This is a first version and it will change as I build and hear back from
people.

## What I looked at

- **Excalidraw**, for how simple a canvas can be: white, minimal, small
  floating toolbars, and how selecting and copy/pasting should feel.
- **FigJam**, for the post-it board idea: notes placed freely, where position
  means something.
- The final project brief's notes on what makes an app good.

## What's here now

- Your own board at `/`, someone else's at `/<username>`.
- Notes you can select (box or Shift-click), drag, copy/paste, duplicate,
  colour and label, with a filter to show only some labels.
- Live cursors of whoever else is on the board, and you can click someone to
  follow them.
